Our story

Old world craft, made right here in Houston.

Since 1964, Nava's has offered superior craftsmanship and exquisite design at affordable prices to home and business owners across Houston.

Our artisans still use traditional hand forged techniques from 17th and 18th century Latin America, so every piece is truly custom.
