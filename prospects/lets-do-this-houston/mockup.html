Meet the founder

One ride in 2015 started it all.

Alan Moore was born in Chicago, raised in Memphis, and graduated from Prairie View A&M with a degree in Social Work. He spent years teaching life skills to at risk youth before a single bike ride through Houston changed his direction.

Today Let’s Do This Houston connects riders with music, local businesses, and the history of 3rd Ward, one route at a time.

“Don’t wait for the perfect time, you will wait forever.”
