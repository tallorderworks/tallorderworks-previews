Our story

More than a century of Texas water wells

O'Day Drilling Company has drilled and serviced water wells in Texas since 1912, and celebrated our 100th anniversary in 2012.

Baylor University honored us with its Founders Award as one of the Top Texas Family Businesses, and Shell Oil Company recognized our crews with its 2011 Safety Award.
