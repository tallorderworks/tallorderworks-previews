Spring Branch, Houston

Food, health, learning and support for families, all under one roof.

Over 16,000 Houstonians a year find help at our center, from early childhood classes and a health clinic to a mobile food pantry and help applying for benefits.

What do you need today?

Our partners work side by side at 8575 Pitner Road, so one visit can connect your family to several kinds of help.

F

Food and benefits

  • Mobile Food Pantry with Houston Food Bank, twice a month
  • Help applying for SNAP, Children's Medicaid and CHIP
  • Family Houston case management
H

Health

  • Spring Branch Community Health Center
  • UTHealth WIC nutrition program
  • Gold Card application help
L

Learning

  • BakerRipley Head Start for ages 3 and 4
  • Family U for parents and children 0 to 3
  • Adult ESL classes with Harris County Department of Education
P

Play

  • Boys and Girls Club after school and summer programs, ages 6 to 17
  • Curiosity Cruiser library visits and story time
  • FitHouston walking group

Built by a community that saw a need

In 1995, members of Chapelwood United Methodist Church and local service providers came together to study what Spring Branch families needed most. Their answer was health care, recreation, social services and education, all under one roof. Today that vision is the Spring Branch Family Development Center.
