Before you hire anyone

Questions to ask any sprinkler contractor

  • Are you licensed with the State of Texas?
  • Will permits be pulled? If not, why?
  • How deep will the pipes be in my yard?
  • How do you solvent weld pipes and fittings?
  • How and why are the stations configured?
  • How do you wire the control valves?
