About us

Tradition and technology, since 1993.

R & I Machine Works was founded in 1993 by Raul and Israel Amaro. The father and son team merged the traditional ways of machining with the newest CNC equipment.

That mix keeps our established clients coming back and draws in new ones who need a trustworthy, efficient shop, often on tight schedules. We have the know how and the equipment to machine parts other shops would turn away.
